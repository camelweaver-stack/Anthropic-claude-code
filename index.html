The core idea

Your rent is already a mortgage payment. The only question is whose.

In Texas, every $850 a month of housing payment supports roughly $100,000 of house — taxes and insurance included. That's not a sales pitch — it's arithmetic you can check on one napkin. Some months renting genuinely wins. Some leases quietly cost a down payment. Our whole job is helping you tell the difference — in plain numbers, before you sign anything.
