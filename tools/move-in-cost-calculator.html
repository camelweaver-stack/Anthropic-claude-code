What will move-in actually cost?

The advertised rent is never the move-in check. Deposits, app fees, admin fees, pet fees, and a prorated first month stack into a number offices rarely total for you until application day. Total it yourself first.

Where the number bends

"Deposit alternatives" are not deposits. Some communities (Willow Crossing among them) offer services like Jetty — a non-refundable monthly or one-time fee in place of a traditional deposit. It lowers today's check but you never see the money again; a standard deposit usually comes back. Run both versions of this calculator before choosing.

Fees are the negotiable part. When the free weeks are fixed, app, admin, and deposit are where month-end flexibility lives — the negotiation cheat sheet has the exact ask.

Watch for add-ons. "Benefit packages," valet trash, and mandatory tech fees appear on some units as monthly line items. Ask for the full itemized monthly cost sheet too, not just move-in.
