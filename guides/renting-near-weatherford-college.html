Renting near Weatherford College

Published by West FW Living, an independent publication of Anastasia Weaver Media · Updated September 3, 2026

Weatherford College doesn't run the big dorm system of a four-year campus, which makes the local rental market the de facto student housing plan — and the math works differently when a roommate, a course schedule, and a nine-month academic year enter the picture.

The realistic options

Closest and cheapest: College Park — 2-bedrooms from $1,200 near campus (1BR pricing wasn't listed this cycle), no concession games, which matters because concession-free base rents don't jump at renewal the way lease-up rates do. The roommate play: a 2-bedroom split two ways beats almost any 1-bedroom — College Park's $1,200 2BR is $600 a head (note the 3.5x income requirement is calculated on the household), and Oxford's 2BR from $1,647 splits to low-$800s — though its August "2 months free" is gone, so requote before you tour. Watchlist worth watching: the 76086 communities near campus — Holland Lake, Cypress View — appear in our 76086 zip page pending full verification.

Three student-specific cautions: screening usually wants 3× income or a guarantor — a parent co-signer changes everything, ask about the guarantor process before paying an app fee (the income math); 12-month leases outlast 9-month academic years, so either match your lease to next summer's plan or read the subletting reality first; and month-end signing timed to semester start can catch a special — they decay fast.
