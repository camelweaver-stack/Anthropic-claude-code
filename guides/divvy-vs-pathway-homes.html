Divvy vs Pathway: the 2026 reality

A year ago this was a features table. Today it starts with a status line — and ends with the three rules every program applicant should carry.

✓ Status compiled from public reporting: late July 2026 — verify both programs directly the week you apply

The comparison that changed this year

A year ago this page would have been a features table. Today it starts with a status line: Divvy has sharply contracted since its Brookfield acquisition — recent reporting says it’s closed to new applications, with earlier coverage describing an inventory-only mode (full status). Pathway, backed by Invitation Homes, is actively operating and now carries the category’s strongest model (full review). So the honest 2026 comparison is less “which is better” than “what Divvy offered, whether Pathway’s version fits you, and what the contraction teaches.”

What the contraction teaches

Both programs are institutional investors whose appetite tracks interest rates — when the math tightens, doors close on the newest applicants first. Three permanent rules follow: verify the week you apply, never pay a third party to “get you in” (real programs charge nothing and prequalify softly), and always price the alternative — twelve months of concession-cheap rent plus deliberate credit repair beats most program premiums for anyone within ~60 points of mortgage-ready. Where your score stands and what your payment supports decide which path is actually yours; the full landscape is in the Texas program table.
