What August means for your leverage

Four communities in one small pocket are running 6–8 weeks free at the same time. That's not generosity — it's competition, and it peaks in late summer when properties fight over the school-year moving wave. Two practical consequences: first, the "biggest" special doesn't automatically win. Eight weeks free at a higher rent can cost more over 12 months than seven weeks at a lower one — add up everything you'd pay, divide by the months, compare. Second, when free weeks are fixed, the negotiable items are deposit, application, and admin fees, and month-end is when leasing offices are most flexible. Ask pleasantly; the worst answer is no.

Concessions like these have an expiration date. When vacancy tightens, the free weeks disappear first — and quietly.
