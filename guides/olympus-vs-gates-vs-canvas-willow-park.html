The honest verdicts

Solo or couple: it’s Olympus vs Gates, and the decision is unit-level, not property-level — with 8 vs 7 weeks free on near-identical sticker rents, the winner is whichever quotes the better effective rent for the specific unit and dates you want. Get both quotes in writing the same week and divide total cost by 12. Family needing bedrooms in Aledo ISD: Canvas vs Willow Crossing is a genuine coin-flip — Canvas buys you a standalone house and real yard at a premium; Willow Crossing counters with lower entry pricing behind a gate. Run both at their conditional-offer numbers and let the arithmetic pick. Everyone: all four sit within two miles of each other, so tour them the same afternoon, collect four written quotes, and negotiate the flexible fees at whichever wins — the cheat sheet is the script.
