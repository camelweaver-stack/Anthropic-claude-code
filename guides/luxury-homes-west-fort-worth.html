Luxury on the west side: where the seven-figure market actually lives

West-side luxury isn't a gated cliché — it's three distinct products: Walsh's custom-and-estate tier (production tops out through the $900s; custom runs $1M–$2M+ on premium lots), Aledo-orbit acreage estates (The Grasslands, Bear Creek Ranch tier — land as the luxury), and the scattered trophy properties along the corridor's creeks and ridgelines.

What luxury buyers here verify that others don't

The tax bill is a second mortgage: at Walsh's 2.689%, a $1.5M home carries ~$3,360/month in taxes alone (the exemption and protest playbook matters more, not less, at this tier — and note the over-65 school-tax ceiling for downsizing buyers). Aledo inventory runs long at the top: ~5–6 months of supply in recent readings means selective buyers and negotiating room — priced-to-comps listings move, aspirational ones sit, and sitting listings are opportunities. Custom means diligence shifts: builder reputation, structural warranty terms, and (on acreage) well/septic/survey lead the inspection stack. And luxury resale competes with build-new: at this tier the ledger runs through architect timelines, not just price.

Neighborhood files for the tier: Walsh · Bear Creek Ranch · The Grasslands · definitive guides: Walsh, Aledo.

Quick answers

Where are the luxury neighborhoods in west Fort Worth?

The estate ridge on Team Ranch Road (Montrachet, Montserrat, La Cantera), the gated country-club community at Mira Vista, Clearfork's walkable-luxury district, acreage luxury at Bella Flora, and the value entry at Westworth Village — each has a pocket file on this site.

How much do luxury homes cost in west Fort Worth?

The band runs from high-six-figure entries in the value pockets to well past seven figures for new customs on the estate ridge. Every pocket file on this site carries the same caveat: this tier trades quietly, so pull live comparables rather than trusting list prices.

Do luxury buyers in Texas still get the homestead exemption?

Yes — the $140,000 school-portion homestead exemption and the 10% appraisal cap apply regardless of price; proportionally they matter less on seven-figure assessments. Parcel-level taxing entities differ street to street on the estate ridge, so the tax lookup is per-parcel work.
