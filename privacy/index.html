Privacy Policy

Last updated August 10, 2026. Plain language, no legalese padding — this is what we actually do with the information you share with us.

West FW Living ("we," "us") publishes an independent field guide to renting and buying on Fort Worth's west side at westfwliving.com. We are not a real estate brokerage. This page explains what information we collect when you use this site, why, and what your options are.

What we collect

We collect information you give us directly:

  • Early-list and inquiry forms — name, email, phone number, and optional details like your target move-in date or notes, submitted through forms on this site.
  • Chat concierge — messages you send through the chat widget, and if you share an email address in that conversation, it's routed to us the same way as a form submission.
  • Text/SMS — if you text the number listed on this site, we receive your phone number and message content through our carrier/messaging provider.

We also collect a small amount of technical information automatically:

  • Attribution data — the page you landed on, the page you were referred from, and any campaign parameters (utm_source, utm_medium, etc.) present in the URL. This is stored temporarily in your browser's session storage (cleared when you close your browser) and, if you submit a form, is included with that submission so we know which page/campaign it came from. This is not a persistent tracking cookie.

We do not currently run Google Analytics or any advertising pixel on this site. If that changes, we'll update this page first.

How we use it

We use the information you submit to respond to your inquiry, send the specials/availability updates you signed up for, and improve which pages and content are useful to visitors. We do not sell your information to third parties.

Who sees it

Form and chat submissions are processed through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) and our site-hosting provider's serverless functions, solely to deliver your message to us. We don't share your contact information with landlords, property managers, or advertisers without your separate, explicit request to be connected with one.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we have about you, ask us to delete it, or unsubscribe from any list at any time — email or text us using the contact info below and we'll take care of it within a reasonable time.

Children

This site and its forms are intended for adults searching for housing. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If our data practices change in a material way, we'll update this page and change the "last updated" date above.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information: text (682) 233-1426 or email leads@anastasiaweaver.com.

This page is a first-draft policy describing our actual current practices. It has not yet been reviewed by an attorney; treat it as accurate-but-informal until a formal legal review is complete.
