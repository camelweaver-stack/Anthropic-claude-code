Three honest footnotes

1. Ownership costs more than the payment. Maintenance is yours, and Texas property taxes rise with values. Budget for both.
2. If you might leave DFW inside ~3 years, renting can genuinely win. I'll say so to your face.
3. The real number comes from a lender. A soft pre-qual takes 10 minutes, doesn't touch your score, and turns "roughly" into "exactly." I'll connect you with one who's fast and won't spam you.

"But I assumed I couldn't qualify"

Most renters who tell me that are running on an assumption, not a denial. FHA loans run 3.5% down with flexible credit; VA loans are 0% down for eligible service members; and down-payment assistance programs exist across Tarrant and Parker County. And if your credit genuinely isn't there yet, rent-to-own programs start around a 550 score. There's a door for almost every situation — the trick is knowing which one is yours.
