Sand Island / Honolulu Harbor · Honolulu County MHA

U.S. Coast Guard Base Honolulu: live on or off?

Sand Island sits inside the working harbor, which makes this the most urban housing problem on the island: town condos, town parking, town prices. The compensation is that Coast Guard members here can live the closest thing Oahu offers to a city life.

The allowance side of the ledger

Your BAH here is the same as every other Oahu installation — one county-wide rate set by rank and dependents. The decision is whether your grade's number covers a pocket you can actually commute from, or whether the on-base waitlist (contact the housing office the day orders drop) is the better bridge.
