Windward · Honolulu County MHA

Marine Corps Base Hawaii — Kaneohe Bay: live on or off?

The windward side is greener, wetter, cooler — and priced like people know it. Kaneohe and Kailua are the two real off-base options; both run above the island's typical bands, with Kailua at a hard premium. Living leeward to save money means commuting the H-3 through the mountain every day, which is beautiful exactly twice.

The allowance side of the ledger

Your BAH here is the same as every other Oahu installation — one county-wide rate set by rank and dependents. The decision is whether your grade's number covers a pocket you can actually commute from, or whether the on-base waitlist (contact the housing office the day orders drop) is the better bridge.
