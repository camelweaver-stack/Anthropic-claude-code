Commute-real for: USCG Sand Island, Fort Shafter, Tripler

Living in Downtown / Kakaako on military orders

Downtown and Kakaako are the city option: high-rise condos, the rail line, third-wave coffee, and the only Oahu lifestyle where a car is optional. Coast Guard members and single service members at the medical and headquarters commands make it work best — against-traffic commutes and walkable everything.

The honest trade

Parking stalls, HOA fees, and pet rules decide more than square footage here; get all three in writing before applying. Street noise is a feature of the genre.

Buyer note. Town towers are where the VA condo-approval list matters most on the entire island — check the project before you check the view. Full math in the VA brief.

For families

Schools

Downtown / Kakaako sits in urban Honolulu, where several district boundaries overlap — look your exact address up in the DOE school finder rather than trust a pocket-level list. The family layer maps schools by gate.

Childcare

Licensed full-time care on Oahu runs roughly $900/mo (median, licensed center or group home) to $1,561/mo (median, center-based infant/toddler) — the crossover math is in The Second Paycheck Report. Find licensed providers through the Hawaii DHS finder or PATCH (808-839-1988); confirm each provider's current license before visiting.

Cost source: 2023 Hawaii Child Care Market Rate Study (Hawaii DHS, CCDF biennial requirement; prepared by PATCH). Pulled 2026-08-05.
