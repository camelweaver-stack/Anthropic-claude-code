Living in Waipahu on military orders

Waipahu delivers the most rent per dollar this close to the harbor: plantation-town roots, dense local neighborhoods, legendary food (start with the okazuya counters), and quick access to both the H-1 and the rail line. Families stretching a junior-enlisted allowance into three bedrooms end up here more than anywhere else on the leeward side.

The honest trade

It's dense and working — smaller lots, busier streets, and quality varies block by block; tour in person and at night.
