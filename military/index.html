BAH counts as qualifying income for a VA loan — and at west-side prices, many E-5+ rents are already mortgage-sized. Zero down, BAH gross-up, and what it buys at real west-side tax rates — then run the rent-vs-own math.

PCS timing vs. specials timing

Two clocks matter. Most weeks-free offers require move-in by month-end, and "look & lease" bonuses expire 24–48 hours after touring — so a PCS window that lands mid-month can catch or miss thousands of dollars depending on which side of the month you sign. If your report date is flexible by even a week, check the current specials before choosing it. And because concessions attach to lease length, a 12-month lease that ends during the next PCS-season summer is worth negotiating for specifically.

Renting sight-unseen from your current duty station? Every community's field notes on this site end with the same rule, and it goes double for remote signing: get the special, the unit, and the itemized fees quoted in one written email before applying.
