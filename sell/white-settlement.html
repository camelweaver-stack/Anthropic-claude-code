What drives the price here

  • Lockheed Martin proximity — a permanent, badge-carrying buyer pool five minutes away
  • The most attainable entry price on the west side, which keeps investor and first-timer demand constant
  • Smaller-lot maintenance appeal for downsizers

What sellers in White Settlement get wrong

  • Investor offers move fast but often below list — know the difference between a fast sale and a full-price one
  • Condition and cleanliness photograph is everything in this band; presentation moves price by whole percents

The 2026 backdrop

The west side is a more patient market than the 2021–22 frenzy stories in your head. Inventory is up, buyers compare harder, and new construction along the corridor competes with resale on incentives, not just price. Homes that are priced to the current comps and presented well still sell in normal time; homes priced to a neighbor's 2022 memory are the ones aging on the portals. The whole game in White Settlement right now is entering at the right number the first week — the data above is where that conversation starts.
