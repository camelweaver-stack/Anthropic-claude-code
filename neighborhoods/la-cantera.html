La Cantera: the gate on Team Ranch

The staffed-gate community at Team Ranch — the third address on the estate ridge, with its own character between its two famous neighbors.

What it is

La Cantera at Team Ranch is the gated community holding the Benbrook-addressed (76126) stretch of the Team Ranch corridor — a staffed entrance, custom and semi-custom homes, and the same ridge topography that defines this pocket family. It rounds out the estate ridge with Montserrat and Montrachet: three gates within a couple of miles, each a different vintage and price posture, which is precisely why they should be toured as a set. La Cantera's niche has generally been the (relatively) accessible entry to the ridge — still firmly luxury money, with the gate and the views, below the newest-construction ceiling next door.

The file

Taxes and services: the Benbrook address means a different city stack than the Fort Worth parcels up the road — one more reason the parcel-level lookup is non-negotiable on this ridge, where taxing entities change at the property line. Schools: verify zoning per address; this seam sits near district boundaries and the private-school set (the file) shops the same drive. Recreation: Benbrook Lake's trails and marina are minutes south — see the lake collection for the honest read on "waterfront" here. Full map: the luxury file.
