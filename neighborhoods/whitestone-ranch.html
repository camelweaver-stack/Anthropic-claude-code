Whitestone Ranch: Benbrook's golf-side family pocket

The Benbrook subdivision family on the Whitestone golf side — attainable family stock minutes from the lake, with the value case Benbrook is quietly known for.

What it is

Whitestone Ranch anchors the subdivision family on Benbrook's southwest golf side (76126, along Whitestone Ranch Rd) — production-built family stock from the 2000s-and-newer waves, priced consistently under comparable Fort Worth neighborhoods east of the loop, with Whitestone's fairways threading the area and Benbrook Lake's trail-and-marina country five minutes south. It's a core exhibit in the Benbrook value case: the same commute band as far-southwest Fort Worth at a genuine discount, traded against Benbrook's smaller-city services menu.

The file

Schools: Benbrook addresses zone to Fort Worth ISD's western campuses — verify the current campus per address, and note the private set's southwest campuses are an easy drive from here. Taxes: Benbrook city + Tarrant + FWISD stack; the playbook covers the exemption filings. The daily-life read: the lake is the amenity — trails, the marina (ramp fees posted), picnic country — with Clearfork fifteen minutes up Bryant Irvin for the dinner tier. Collections: golf · water.
