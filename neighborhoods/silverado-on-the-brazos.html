Silverado on the Brazos: river-country acreage

Gated acreage estates above the Brazos south of Weatherford — the corridor's clearest 'upscale country living' proposition, with the river as the amenity.

What it is

Silverado on the Brazos (Silverado Dr, south of Weatherford off the FM 1885/Tin Top corridor) is the gated acreage community built along the Brazos River bluffs — large building sites and custom homes, an on-site clubhouse scene, and the kind of scenery ("a little piece of heaven" is the recurring local verdict) that the flat side of the county can't offer. This is the file for the buyer whose plan says land, view, gate in that order: sites here are bought as much for the building envelope and the river country as for any structure standing on them. Pricing spans from lot-only purchases through seven-figure completed customs; the market is thin and quiet, so comparables need pulling fresh.

The honest logistics file

Everything in the Canyon West file's frontier checklist applies here with a river twist: verify the district for the exact parcel (this stretch runs near the Brock ISD line — the hub), price the true commute (Tin Top Rd to town, then the highway), confirm well/septic/propane per site, run the internet check before assuming remote work is viable, and — river-specific — review the floodplain map and any river-access easements during the option period.
