Aledo High School: the mover's briefing

≈1,731 students; the district's flagship and the reason 'Aledo' is a brand name in Texas — verify programs and any capacity planning directly (a 2025–26 Growth Committee just presented high-school space recommendations to the board).

What we publish vs. what you verify

We publish structure — where campuses sit, how the feeder pattern runs, what the district's growth means for boundaries. We deliberately don't publish ratings-site scores or teacher chatter: ratings lag, staff change, and boundaries in a district adding rooftops this fast get redrawn. The verification that actually protects you: ask Aledo ISD for the zoned campus of the exact address, in writing, before your option period ends — and ask whether any rezoning studies touch that area. Enrollment follows residence, so a lease secures a campus the same as a deed (the rent-the-district play).
