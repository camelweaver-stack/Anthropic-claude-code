Vandagriff Elementary: the in-town campus

K–5 on FM 5 — one of the district's largest elementaries, the campus file, and the rezoning caveat that comes with in-town growth.

The campus file

Vandagriff Elementary · K–5 · 151 FM 5 South, Aledo · 817-441-8771 · school hours 7:40am–3:10pm · Principal: Jake Bean. This is one of the district's larger elementary campuses — enrollment has run in the 600s with a student-teacher ratio around 16:1 per state data — sitting in the town-of-Aledo core near the FM 1187 corridor, and sharing the district's single secondary funnel to Aledo Middle / McAnally, Daniel Ninth Grade, and Aledo High.

What the address side means

Vandagriff's FM 5 location makes it the in-town campus — the zone that has historically covered the original Aledo core and the subdivisions growing south of downtown. Because it's one of the larger campuses in a fast-growing district, it sits closest to the rezoning conversation each cycle: verify the current attendance zone for the exact address before signing anything (the five-minute check). New-construction shoppers weighing which communities feed which campuses should pair this file with the Aledo new-construction guide and the neighborhood library.
