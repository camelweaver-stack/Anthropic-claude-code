White Settlement ISD: the campus hub

All eight campuses with addresses, the district's real 2024 state rating, and what that means if you're weighing White Settlement against the rest of the corridor.

The campus directory, verified

White Settlement ISD serves roughly 7,000 students out of the district office at 8224 White Settlement Rd. High school: Brewer High School (1025 W. Loop 820 N., Fort Worth). Middle: Brewer Middle School (1000 S. Cherry Lane, White Settlement). Elementary: West Early Learners Academy — Pre-K/K (8900 Oels St., White Settlement), Liberty Elementary (7976 Whitney Drive, White Settlement), Fine Arts Academy at Tannahill (701 American Flyer Blvd., Fort Worth), North Elementary STEAM Academy (9850 Legacy Drive, Fort Worth), and Blue Haze Elementary (601 Blue Haze Drive, Fort Worth). Alternative: RISE Alternative Campus (8500 Rockway St., White Settlement). Source: WSISD's own campus directory, retrieved August 2026 — verify current zoning for any specific address directly with the district, as boundaries and campus assignments can change year to year.

How movers should read the district

WSISD received an overall C rating in the state's 2024 accountability system (C on Student Achievement, C on School Progress, C on Closing the Gaps), per the Texas Education Agency's accountability data. That's a genuinely different academic profile from Aledo ISD's top-decile posture or Weatherford ISD's near-regional-average performance — worth knowing plainly rather than discovering after a lease or purchase. It doesn't mean every campus performs identically; TEA publishes campus-level ratings separately, and it's worth checking the specific campus your address zones to before deciding. For the area itself — cost, rent-or-buy math, and how it stacks up to a neighboring option — see the rent-or-buy read for White Settlement and the Benbrook-vs-White-Settlement comparison. One important correction while we're here: "Benbrook ISD" isn't a real district — homes in Benbrook are zoned to Fort Worth ISD, not a separate Benbrook-named district. If you've seen that term used elsewhere (including previously on this site), treat it as an error; we'll cover the actual FWISD campuses serving Benbrook in a future update.
