Weatherford ISD: the campus hub

All eleven campuses with addresses, the big-district program case, and the boundary seam every Hudson Oaks shopper should verify.

The campus directory, verified

Weatherford ISD serves roughly 8,000 students across the district's ~254 square miles. High school: Weatherford High School (2121 Bethel Rd) with the separate WHS Ninth Grade Center (1007 S. Main). Middle: Joe M. Tison Middle School (102 Meadowview Rd) and Shirley Hall Middle School (823 S. Bowie Dr). Elementary (7): Stephen F. Austin (1776 Texas Dr), David Crockett (1015 Jameson St), Raymond E. Curtis (501 W. Russell), Bose Ikard (100 Ikard Ln), Mary Martin (719 N. Oakridge Dr — the campus on the Hudson Oaks side), Juan N. Seguin (499 E. Eighth St), and Bill W. Wright (1309 W. Charles St). Pre-K programming runs through Crockett. The district publishes an address-lookup tool for zoning — use it per address, per year, as boundaries adjust with growth.

How movers should read the district

WISD is the corridor's big-district option: a full 6A-scale program menu (AP, dual credit with Weatherford College across town, career-and-technical tracks) at Weatherford's lower price of entry — the same town where the total property-tax rate is the corridor's lowest at 1.902% and the rent-or-buy math runs friendliest. State test performance has tracked near regional averages — a genuinely different profile from Aledo ISD's top-decile posture, traded against meaningfully lower housing costs. The relevant campus for the Hudson Oaks/Willow Park boundary shopper is Mary Martin on Oakridge Dr; addresses in that seam split between WISD and Aledo ISD street by street, which is exactly why the zone-verification habit matters most there.
