How to actually get the full deal

Timing: most offers require move-in before month-end — the same special quietly shrinks mid-month.
Lease length: the biggest concessions attach to 12–14 month terms; sometimes a longer term unlocks more.
Stacking: if the free weeks are fixed, the negotiable items are deposit, app fees, and admin fees — it never hurts to ask, especially near month-end.
The math check: 8 weeks free on a $1,319 2-bedroom is ≈ $2,430 in your pocket — but only if the net-effective rent still beats the competing property. Divide total lease cost by 12 for each option before deciding; the "bigger" special doesn't automatically win.
