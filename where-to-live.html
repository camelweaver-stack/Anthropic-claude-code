How the ranking works

Budget and hard constraints carry the most weight. Then the engine scores commute fit, home type, and only the lifestyle priorities you selected. A community can still appear with a lower score when it misses a preference so you can see the trade-off rather than getting a black-box answer.

Use the results as a shortlist. Then verify the exact property — particularly school attendance zones, tax entities, HOA/PID assessments and drive time at your actual commute hour.
