# MEMO — PCS Oahu Compliance Fork: Publisher Mode vs. Sponsored Service Mode
For: Chris / Anastasia Weaver · From: PCS Oahu build desk · August 2026 · One page, one decision.

## Current posture (live today)
pcsoahu.com operates in **publisher mode**: educational content and email capture only, no
offers of brokerage, leasing, or advisory services, no agent identification, "full service
coming soon." Lead routing: forms → leads@anastasiaweaver.com, tagged referral-hi-oahu-pcs, for
referral through Anastasia's Hawaii brokerage to partner agents. This posture was inherited from
the Texas-portfolio standing spec, where it is mandatory pending TX licensure.

## The fork
Unlike the Texas sites, **Anastasia's Hawaii license is active now.** That makes a second
posture legally available for this one site, if — and only if — her Hawaii brokerage formally
sponsors the site's advertising: **sponsored service mode**, where the site may carry
service-language CTAs ("request a personalized neighborhood plan," "schedule a relocation
consultation," "request a sell-vs-rent analysis"), identify Anastasia as the licensed
professional behind the platform (the roadmap's agent-as-guide positioning, plus the E-E-A-T
byline that is the site's single biggest deferred ranking lever), and eventually carry listings.

## What the brokerage needs to confirm before any flip
1. Written sponsorship of pcsoahu.com as advertising of the brokerage, and the exact license
   identification / brokerage-name display Hawaii law and brokerage policy require on the site.
2. Approval of the specific CTA wording (draft list above; final copy supplied on request).
3. The referral/compensation structure for leads the site generates directly vs. referred out
   (the current broker-to-broker referral structure under the RESPA §8 exemption presumably
   continues for partner-agent handoffs; brokerage to confirm treatment of direct service).
4. The SEQ-PCS email AGENT-handoff wording (separate review copy already in this folder).
5. Advertising-record and supervision requirements the brokerage imposes (retention, review).

## What does NOT change under either posture
No lender placements or recommendations, ever. No school rankings, no crime/safety content, no
steering. Dated, sourced data with verify-independently language. Equal Housing Opportunity
throughout. These are house rules, not licensing artifacts.

## Mechanics and timing
The flip is a generator constants change (CTA blocks + byline/Person schema are pre-designed,
gate-enforced) — roughly a one-day build once approvals exist. Value timing: the byline should
ideally be live before the **December BAH-cycle pitch wave**, when the site gets its largest
press and backlink exposure of the year. Decision requested by **mid-November 2026**.

## If the answer is no or not yet
Nothing is lost: the site continues compounding in publisher mode, and the fork stays open. The
only cost of waiting is the E-E-A-T gap against competitor sites fronted by named licensees.
