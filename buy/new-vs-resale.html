THE CORRIDOR'S CENTRAL BUYING QUESTION

New build vs. resale in west Fort Worth, decided by math

With incentives factored in, new construction has moved to near price-parity with resale across DFW — which means the decision now turns on the costs each path hides, not the sticker. Here's the honest ledger for this corridor.

The ledger

New constructionResale
Entry pricingDFW new builds from the mid-$290s; corridor entries from $360,990 (Morningstar)Fort Worth median ≈ $335–338K; corridor varies by pocket
The sweetener$10K–$40K incentive packages, buydowns to ~4.99% (Index)Price negotiation + seller concessions; ~3.5 months FW supply = real leverage
The hidden addsDesign-center overages averaging 12–17% of price; lot premiums; year-two tax jump of 30–50% at full assessmentDeferred maintenance risk — the Texas classic is foundation work, $10K–$50K+; aging HVAC/roof
Supply picture≈5.2 months DFW new-home supply — builder-motivated territory≈3.5 months in Fort Worth — balanced, not desperate
Contract termsBuilder-drafted (not TREC forms); option-period protections often reshapedStandard TREC contract, full option period
Legal backstopBuilder warranty + Texas RCLA/DTPA procedures (follow the notice rules or lose them)Seller's disclosure + your inspection

How to actually decide

Price both paths all-in, not sticker-to-sticker: new build = base + realistic upgrades + lot premium + full-assessment taxes − incentive value; resale = price + a maintenance reserve honest about the home's age (an inspection quantifies it). Run each through the calculator at the right tax rate — a Walsh new build and a Weatherford resale differ by 0.8 points of tax before anything else. Inspect both: new construction gets third-party inspections too (pre-drywall and final at minimum); "it's new" is a warranty, not a guarantee. And read the leverage correctly: aged spec inventory is where builders bend most; tired listings past 60 days are where resale sellers do. In a corridor where $160M+ of future supply is filed, patience is currently paid.
