FOR THE NAS JRB & LOCKHEED CORRIDOR

VA loans in Fort Worth: the west-side edition

With NAS JRB anchoring the west side and Lockheed next door, the VA loan is this corridor's quiet superpower: zero down, no monthly mortgage insurance, competitive rates — and BAH counts as qualifying income. Here's the benefit explained straight, matched against what it actually buys out here.

How the benefit works

Zero down is real — with full entitlement there's no VA-imposed loan limit in most cases, and no down payment required. No PMI ever, which against a conventional low-down loan saves real money monthly. The trade is the funding fee: a one-time charge (roughly 2.15% at zero-down first use, higher on subsequent uses, commonly financed into the loan) — waived entirely for buyers with a service-connected disability rating, which makes the VA loan nearly unbeatable for them. Eligibility: generally 90 continuous days active duty (you don't wait for separation), longer thresholds for some service eras and Guard/Reserve; your Certificate of Eligibility settles it. BAH counts as income — and it's tax-free, so many lenders "gross it up," raising your qualifying power beyond the sticker amount. Texas adds its own layer: the Texas Veterans Land Board home-loan program can offer competitive-rate financing to Texas veterans and pairs with (or substitutes for) the federal benefit — worth a comparison quote via the official VLB site.

Three corridor-specific cautions

1. Builder incentives and VA loans coexist — flex cash works with VA financing, but "preferred lender" requirements still apply to the incentive; compare that lender's VA pricing against an outside VA specialist before assuming the package is a gift. 2. The appraisal has teeth: VA appraisals enforce minimum property requirements — usually a non-issue on new construction, occasionally a negotiation on older resale; it protects you. 3. Separation math: BAH stops at separation, and qualifying on BAH you're about to lose is how service members get overextended — if you're inside two years of a decision point, run the affordability numbers on post-service income too (the calculator makes it a two-minute check). Not lending advice — a VA-experienced lender runs your real numbers; this page just makes sure you walk in knowing the shape of the benefit.
