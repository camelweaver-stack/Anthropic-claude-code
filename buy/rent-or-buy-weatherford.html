The honest read for Weatherford

The Weatherford-specific read: this is where buying pencils earliest in the corridor. Lower entry prices, the ~1.90% rate saving roughly $63/month per $400K versus the Aledo-ISD side, and a rental floor low enough that saving a down payment while renting here is genuinely fast. The catch is the same coin's other face: the deepest rent deal we track — Oxford's "2 months free" netting to the mid-$900s — makes year-one renting hard for ownership to beat on pure monthly cash. Run the horizon honestly: under ~3 years, take the concession; over 5, the tax arbitrage compounds for owners.
