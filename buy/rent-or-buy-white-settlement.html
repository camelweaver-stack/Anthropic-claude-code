The honest read for White Settlement / West FW

The west-FW-specific read: renters get the corridor's best value-per-square-foot (1,017 sq ft one-bedrooms); owners pay the Fort Worth-in-Parker rate (~2.35% where Aledo-zoned) — the corridor's highest, which pushes the buy break-even up. Two factors bend it back: the VA loan — this is NAS JRB's backyard, and zero-down changes the cash math completely (the west-side VA guide computes it) — and entry prices below the corridor's Aledo-side floor. Do the two-visit noise homework before either decision this close to the field.
