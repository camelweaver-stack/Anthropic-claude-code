The honest read for Benbrook

The Benbrook-specific read: the absence matters — no tracked lease-up means no 8-weeks-free games, so Benbrook rents run closer to their true level while the I-20 corridor's are temporarily discounted. That compresses Benbrook's rent-vs-buy gap: established resale stock (with the inspection homework older homes deserve — foundation, HVAC age), Fort Worth-area tax basis (~2.2–2.4% depending on the taxing units — verify per address), and lake-adjacent lifestyle that resale buyers pay for and renters mostly can't access. For corridor renters eyeing Benbrook: compare against your post-concession renewal rent, not your discounted year-one rate — that's the number Benbrook ownership actually competes with.
