# PITCH KIT — The BAH Reality Report link outreach
Purpose: earn citations and backlinks to /bah-report/, the site's ranking engine. Fire the full
wave the day new BAH rates drop (December); run trickle outreach year-round when the report
refreshes. Tone rules apply to outreach exactly as to the site: respectful, factual, zero
hooah-kitsch, no pandering. We are offering data, not asking a favor.

## Target list (build contacts before December)
**Military money & PCS publishers:** military personal-finance sites and PCS-content publishers
that cover BAH annually and cite sources (the sites that write "new BAH rates announced" stories
every December need exactly the rents-vs-rates angle they never have).
**Spouse and family bloggers/podcasts:** Hawaii-PCS storytellers with real audiences; offer the
report as the numbers behind the story they already tell.
**Local press:** Honolulu housing/economy reporters (Star-Advertiser, Civil Beat, Pacific
Business News) — the angle is local: "the military allowance that anchors a big slice of Oahu's
rental market just changed; here's where it clears the market and where it doesn't."
**Community (citations, not links):** base-community Facebook groups and relevant subreddits —
share the report as a resource, never as promotion; follow each community's self-promo rules and
lead with the answer, not the URL.
**Institutional:** installation newcomer pages and family-support newsletters sometimes link
civilian resources; a short, neutral note to the family-support center is worth one email a year.

## Rules
- Never offer payment, exchanges, or reciprocal links. Never use service language (publisher
  mode applies off-site too). Never claim endorsement by DoD or any service.
- One follow-up maximum, a week later, shorter than the original. Silence means no.
- Log every send/response so the December wave compounds year over year.
