Commuting from Gates at Meadow Place

Gates at Meadow Place (Meadow Place Dr, Willow Park) sits in the Willow Park pocket off I-20 — close enough to Fort Worth's west-side employers that this is one of the few Parker County addresses where the "small-town living, city paycheck" pitch survives contact with a Tuesday morning.

Gates shares the Meadow Place pocket with Canvas — same freeway access, same drive math. If the two are neck-and-neck on price for you, the commute won't break the tie; the net-effective math will.

The one commute rule we push on every page: drive it yourself, at your actual departure time, before you sign. Every mapping app will do a "depart at 7:10 a.m. Tuesday" simulation — ten minutes of homework against a 12-month commitment.

Working at Lockheed or NAS JRB?

You're the reason this pocket is competitive. The plant and the base sit side-by-side on Fort Worth's west edge, which puts Willow Park upstream of the worst congestion — you're driving toward the city's edge, not through the city.
