Aledo vs Weatherford

The corridor's classic trade: the school-district premium against the county seat's price relief.

The verdict, up front

This is the corridor's fundamental trade stated as two towns: Aledo sells the A-rated single-track district and the small-town identity around it, at a premium on both price and tax rate. Weatherford answers with the corridor's lowest total tax rate (1.902%), the lowest entry prices, real city services, and a bigger district with a bigger program menu. Families optimizing for the district pick Aledo and know what they're paying; value optimizers and empty nesters keep landing in Weatherford.
