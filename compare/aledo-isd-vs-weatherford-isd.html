Aledo ISD vs Weatherford ISD

The seam's real question: the top-decile single-track vs the big-district menu at a discount.

The verdict, up front

Aledo ISD is the corridor's engine: A-rated posture, one funnel from seven elementaries to one high school, and a premium priced into every zoned address. Weatherford ISD counters with scale — eleven campuses, AP and dual-credit with Weatherford College, CTE tracks — wrapped in the corridor's lowest housing costs and tax rate. Performance data favors Aledo; the affordability math favors Weatherford; the street-by-street seam through Willow Park and Hudson Oaks means some families get to choose with one move. Verify the exact address's zone before deciding anything.
