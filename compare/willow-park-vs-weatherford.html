Willow Park vs Weatherford

Published by West FW Living, an independent publication of Anastasia Weaver Media · Updated September 25, 2026

The proximity play against the price play, ten miles apart on I-20.

The verdict, up front

This is the corridor’s price-vs-proximity ladder in one matchup. Willow Park sits ten-plus minutes closer to Fort Worth, built itself a downtown (The District) and a medical cluster, and most addresses zone to A-rated Aledo ISD — all of which is priced into both the houses and the ~2.09% tax stack. Weatherford answers with the corridor’s lowest published stack (~1.90%), its lowest entry prices, and real county-seat city services. Buyers optimizing for the district and the drive pick Willow Park and pay for it; buyers optimizing dollars-per-square-foot keep landing in Weatherford.

Quick answers

Is Willow Park or Weatherford better?

Willow Park sells proximity: ten-plus minutes closer to Fort Worth, the new District downtown, the Crown Pointe medical cluster, and Aledo ISD zoning for most addresses — at a premium on both price and tax rate. Weatherford sells price: the corridor’s lowest published tax stack, its lowest entry prices, and full county-seat city services. The side-by-side on this page puts the verified numbers next to each other.

What are the property taxes in Willow Park vs Weatherford?

Willow Park: 2.091% total on the 2024–25 published stack (city 0.4325, Aledo ISD side). Weatherford: 1.902% — the corridor’s lowest. Both cities and both school districts have already adopted flat-or-lower 2026–27 rates. Rates are parcel-specific — always pull the exact address’s taxing entities before contract.
