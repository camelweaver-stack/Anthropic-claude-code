Walsh vs Morningstar

The corridor's two master plans: the flagship ecosystem vs the value alternative.

The verdict, up front

Same district, same I-20 spine, opposite philosophies. Walsh is the full-ecosystem bet — its own elementary, market, gig fiber, amenity campus — carried by the corridor's highest tax stack (2.353% plus plan assessments) and its master-plan price ceiling. Morningstar delivers the recognizable master-plan life — pools, parks, Aledo ISD — with resale depth and more house per dollar. The honest question isn't which is better; it's whether Walsh's ecosystem is worth its delta for how your family actually lives.
