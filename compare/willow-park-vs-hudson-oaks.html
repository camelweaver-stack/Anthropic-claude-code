Willow Park vs Hudson Oaks

The seam decision: two towns sharing one interchange, split by a school-district line and two different tax philosophies.

The verdict, up front

Same interchange, different bets: Willow Park built a downtown and a medical cluster and taxes like it; Hudson Oaks lets its retail strip pay the city's bills and runs one of the area's lightest tax lines. The school seam is the real decision — Aledo ISD vs Weatherford ISD splits these towns street by street, so the district you're buying decides the winner before the towns do.
