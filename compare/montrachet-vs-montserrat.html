Montrachet vs Montserrat

Two gates, one ridge: the new flagship vs the established original.

The verdict, up front

Same Team Ranch ridge, same views, two vintages. Montrachet is the current flagship — guarded gate, amenity program, builder roster, and the newest-construction premium that comes with all three. Montserrat is the ridge's original: mature landscaping, a settled build-out, and a resale market Montrachet won't have for years. Buyers wanting the newest everything tour Montrachet first; buyers allergic to construction cycles and builder queues usually end up happier behind Montserrat's gate. Tour both — they're two minutes apart.
