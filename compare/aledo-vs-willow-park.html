Aledo vs Willow Park

Published by West FW Living, an independent publication of Anastasia Weaver Media · Updated August 18, 2026

Same district, different lives: the in-town identity vs the interchange convenience.

The verdict, up front

Most addresses in both zones feed the same Aledo ISD ladder — so this one isn't a schools decision, it's a lifestyle one. Aledo is the identity purchase: the downtown, the Friday-night civic calendar, the trail system. Willow Park is the convenience purchase: I-20 at the door, The District's restaurant row, the medical cluster, five saved minutes on every commute. Taxes are nearly a wash (2.056 vs 2.091). Buy Aledo for the town, Willow Park for the logistics.
