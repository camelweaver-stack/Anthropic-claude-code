Hudson Oaks vs Weatherford

Published by West FW Living, an independent publication of Anastasia Weaver Media · Updated September 25, 2026

The lightest tax line against the lowest prices, same school district.

The verdict, up front

These two share Weatherford ISD, so the schools mostly cancel out and the trade is pure economics and scale. Hudson Oaks runs one of the area’s lightest tax lines (~1.32% median effective, per county data) because its retail strip pays the city’s bills, and it sits ten-plus minutes closer to Fort Worth — but it’s a small town with established stock and selective new construction. Weatherford is the real city: the corridor’s lowest entry prices, the full spread of housing, county-seat services and a hospital — at a ~1.90% published stack and a longer drive. More house for the money in Weatherford; lighter carrying costs and a shorter commute in Hudson Oaks.

Quick answers

Is Hudson Oaks or Weatherford better?

They share Weatherford ISD, so the trade is economics and scale: Hudson Oaks pairs one of the area’s lightest tax lines with a shorter Fort Worth drive in a small town; Weatherford pairs the corridor’s lowest entry prices with full county-seat services at a longer drive. The side-by-side on this page puts the verified numbers next to each other.

What are the property taxes in Hudson Oaks vs Weatherford?

Hudson Oaks: ~1.32% median effective (county data) — one of the lightest lines out here. Weatherford: 1.902% published total on the 2024–25 stack, the corridor’s lowest full stack. Rates are parcel-specific — always pull the exact address’s taxing entities before contract.
