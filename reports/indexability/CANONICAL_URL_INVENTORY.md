## Classification notes

- `/404.html` — non-public: Netlify serves it with HTTP 404 status for missing routes; correctly excluded from sitemap.
- `/embed/bah-widget.html` — intentional_noindex: standalone iframe widget, `meta robots noindex`, excluded from sitemap, gate-enforced.
- All other pages are canonical public pages: self-canonical to the apex-host `.html`/directory form, `lang=en`, no hreflang (site is English-only by design; the gate fails any page carrying hreflang).
- Netlify Pretty URLs additionally serves each flat `.html` page at its extensionless twin (e.g. `/guides/harpta`), and rewrites internal hrefs to that form at deploy time. Both forms return 200 and carry the identical self-referential canonical pointing at the `.html` form — see SITEMAP_AUDIT.md and the duplicate-content section of the matrix.
