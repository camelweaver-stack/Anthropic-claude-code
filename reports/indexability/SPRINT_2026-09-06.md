# Indexing-Recovery & Conversion Sprint — 2026-09-06

## Root-cause diagnosis
1. **No crawl/index block exists.** Re-verified live this sprint: all 62 (now 63) sitemap URLs
   return 200, self-canonical, no meta/header noindex, no robots blocks, hard 404s, no redirect
   chains, all content static HTML. Restored guides from the 08-24 audit remain live and stable.
2. **Homepage-as-catch-all is architectural + age-driven.** Google's proven queries map to intents
   the site served one level down with brand-led titles ("BAH Reality Report", "TLA"), no master
   PCS hub existed at all (biggest gap: 4 of the top 6 queries are PCS-to-Hawaii intent with no
   dedicated page), base pages had exactly one internal inbound link each, and the domain is ~5
   weeks old. Homepage position 38 with 4–6 impressions/day is index-selection behavior on a young
   site whose interior pages gave Google weak distinct-purpose signals.
3. **One severe non-SEO production defect found (Phase 2): the live lead form posted to the
   cleartext email endpoint with no honeypot and no consent checkbox.** The 2026-08-10 security
   fix only ever landed on the editorial branch; the production branch's gate literally asserted
   the cleartext endpoint. Every production page served the unprotected form since 2026-08-16.
