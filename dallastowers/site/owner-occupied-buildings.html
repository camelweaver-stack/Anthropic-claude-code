What the roll shows § 03

Across the 13 buildings here with certified county files — 1,132 unit accounts — median assessed values run $419,180 (The Bonaventure) to $3,594,710 (The Residences at the Stoneleigh). Owner-occupancy spans 81–98%. The group recorded 94 deed transfers in the trailing twelve months.

Against the full registry — 61 county-filed buildings, median assessed $552,530, median unit 1,646 sf, median owner-occupancy 77% — this group sits above on value with a larger typical unit at 2,073 sf.

How this list is built § M

Homestead exemption share per building, Dallas CAD 2026 certified roll. Policies change by board vote and rolls change yearly — treat this as the verified starting point, and confirm against current documents before contracting.
