The corridor § 01

No street in Texas holds more named condominium buildings than the two miles of Turtle Creek Boulevard between Blackburn and the Katy Trail's northern bend. The corridor built in three waves: the midcentury pioneers (3525 in 1957, Gold Crest and 21 Turtle Creek in the early '60s), the full-service '80s generation (the Warrington, Claridge, Beverly, La Tour), and the formal European turn at the millennium (the Mayfair, Vendome, Plazas, Renaissance). County records give the corridor's character a number: across its buildings, homestead rates average ~68% — by the public record, these are predominantly residents' towers.

The corridor's quiet phenomenon is combination: at nearly every pre-1990 address, current tax accounts run below original unit counts as owners bought their neighbors — the Claridge alone has absorbed roughly a tenth of its original homes. Bigger homes, fewer doors, steadier boards.

Living here § 03

The lifestyle case is the creek itself: Turtle Creek Park and the linear greenbelt, the Katy Trail's multiple corridor entrances, and Reverchon Park's fields, with the Oak Lawn and Knox dining districts bracketing each end. See the community file for verified events and park programming.
