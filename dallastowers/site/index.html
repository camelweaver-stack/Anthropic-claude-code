    A registry, not a portal

    The big listing sites can show you what's for sale today. They can’t tell you which stack faces the park, what to verify in the bylaws before you buy to lease, or how one tower’s dues compare with the next — the record a buyer actually needs.

    Dallas Towers compiles that record building by building — from HOA documents, county appraisal rolls, and recorded deed transfers — and keeps it current monthly. Free to read, always.
