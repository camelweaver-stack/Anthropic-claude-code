Reading this market § 02

What moves price here

Trail and park adjacency is the premium. Katy Trail–facing units clear $75–$120/sf above identical plans across the street, and Klyde Warren proximity does the same at the district's south end.

Sundance Square walkability is the constant premium: units within two blocks of the square clear $40–$60/sf over the district edge.

District FAQ § 03

Why are downtown Fort Worth condos so much cheaper than Dallas?

Most of the inventory is early-2000s office and warehouse conversions rather than ground-up luxury towers, and Fort Worth’s condo buyer pool is thinner. You get true high-rise living from the low $200s/sf — the tradeoff is older building systems and fewer full-service amenities.

What should I check before buying in a converted building?

Reserve studies and assessment history matter more here than anywhere else in the Metroplex. Conversion-era elevators, plumbing risers, and facades come due at 20–25 years — exactly now. The building files flag each property’s record.

Can I rent out a unit downtown?

Most buildings allow leasing with 6–12 month minimums; two restrict it. Short-term rentals are prohibited by rule in every tracked building downtown, and the city’s STR registration rules apply on top.

Community & everyday life § 04

Downtown's social calendar centers on Sundance Square — free plaza programming year-round, artisan markets at the Sundance Pavilion, and Bass Performance Hall as the permanent home of the symphony, ballet, opera, and the Van Cliburn competition. Each April the Main Street Arts Festival takes over 18 blocks (Texas' largest arts festival, 200+ juried artists) alongside the Fort Worth Art Fair on the plaza — residents should expect street closures the full week. The community file carries the calendar, parking notes, and how to plug into HOA and district meetings.
