The building § 01

The Warrington is a named condominium tower in Turtle Creek, Dallas. The building dates to 1981. It comprises 24 floors and 125 residences.

An HKS-designed 1981 tower of 133 residences at 3831 Turtle Creek with a reputation as the corridor's pied-à-terre building — D Magazine quipped that 'no one really knows anyone who lives there permanently'; VIP tenants have included Michael Jordan and Erik Jonsson, and it remains the classic address-in-waiting while an estate renovation finishes.
