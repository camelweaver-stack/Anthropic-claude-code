Every correction and dataset update, in public. Registries die of staleness; this page is the pulse.

FHA approval layer + photo round 3 Aug 2026

Every county-tracked building now shows its FHA condominium-approval status, checked against HUD’s active-approvals database for Dallas and Tarrant counties: three registry buildings are approved — the Metropolitan (1200 Main), Texas & Pacific Lofts, and Buzz Condos — and the rest carry a plan-on-conventional-financing note. Approvals expire; verify current status with HUD. A systematic 80-building imagery sweep added one rights-clean photograph (1999 McKinney, CC BY) and rejected five false matches; openly licensed coverage now stands at ten buildings.

FEMA flood-zone layer Aug 2026

All geocodable buildings (73) now carry a FEMA National Flood Hazard Layer zone on their intelligence file. Four Turtle Creek-corridor buildings sit in mapped Zone AE — the Warrington, the Renaissance, Parkside, and Mansion Residences — where lenders typically require flood insurance; the other 69 map to Zone X. Point-of-address queries; formal determinations govern.

Correction #20 — combination-index source data Aug 2026

An internal data-restoration gap dropped original-marketed-unit counts from the working dataset; combination figures were re-verified against campaign research and restored for five buildings (3525 Turtle Creek’s 200→93 now the corridor’s deepest absorption on file), with an automated census guard added so silent field loss now fails the build. Buildings without a verified original count simply omit the figure.

Q3 Tower Report + Museum Tower dues Aug 2026

First quarterly Dallas Tower Report published from the certified roll. Museum Tower HOA rate verified at ~$1.34/sf/mo from a 2026 listing disclosure — the flagship's carrying cost now computes to roughly $8,900/month at the median residence.

Dues sweep + city pages rebuilt Aug 2026

Numeric dues rates added for seven more buildings - Twenty-One Turtle Creek ($0.88/sf, consistent across three 2026 listing disclosures), 3525 Turtle Creek, and the Claridge listing-verified; Live Oak, Grand Treviso, Lake Cliff, and Lone Star derived from previously verified monthly ranges and labeled approximate. Carrying-cost panels now render on each. Dallas and Fort Worth city pages rebuilt from the dataset, replacing early placeholder cards and unverified district statistics with county-computed medians and working links.
