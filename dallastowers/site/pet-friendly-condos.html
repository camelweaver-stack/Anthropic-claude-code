What the roll shows § 03

Across the 3 buildings here with certified county files — 223 unit accounts — median assessed values run $250,665 (Live Oak Lofts) to $1,220,940 (Gold Crest). Owner-occupancy spans 73–92%. The group recorded 19 deed transfers in the trailing twelve months.

Against the full registry — 61 county-filed buildings, median assessed $552,530, median unit 1,646 sf, median owner-occupancy 77% — this group sits above on value with a larger typical unit at 2,073 sf.

County files are pending for Forest Park Tower, Houston Place Lofts, Le Bijou; those rows carry registry data only until the next roll pass.

How this list is built § M

Drawn from each building's verified policy field — published rules, listing disclosures, and district sources — not assumptions. Limits on count, size, and breed are the norm even in yes-pet buildings. Policies change by board vote and rolls change yearly — treat this as the verified starting point, and confirm against current documents before contracting.
