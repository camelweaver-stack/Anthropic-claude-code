# Original photography plan — 2026-08-28

Competitor visuals: HAR/brokerage pages use MLS listing photos; official sites
use professional developer photography; the A/B-class buildings' SERP pages
often have poor or stock-adjacent exteriors. Original photography is a real
differentiator and removes any temptation toward stock imagery (prohibited by
the media rights schema: no generic photo may represent a specific tower).

Priority = ranking opportunity × commercial importance × competitor visual
weakness. Shot list per building: exterior from street, entrance, streetscape
context, neighborhood/skyline context; amenities only with legitimate access.
