West FW rentals with attached garages

"Garage available" on a listing site can mean a detached rental unit across the lot for $150 a month. This page covers the two tracked communities where an attached two-car garage comes with every home — and what that convenience costs against a standard apartment.

What the garage is really worth

In Parker County the garage isn't a luxury line item — it's hail insurance, an August car interior below 130°, and storage that keeps a 3-bedroom feeling like a 3-bedroom. When apartments do offer detached garages, they typically run $100–175/month extra where available. Fold that into your comparison: an apartment at $1,319 plus a rented garage stops being obviously cheaper than a townhome whose garage is already in the rent — run both through the weeks-free calculator and compare net-effective to net-effective.

The deeper choice between the two garage communities — house-with-yard vs. gated-townhome — gets its own honest breakdown on the townhomes & houses page.
