Know the zip before you search it

Zip codes out here cross city AND school-district lines — 76087 spans Willow Park, Hudson Oaks & the Weatherford I-20 corridor, which means two listings in "the same zip" can sit in different districts with different tax bases and different commutes. Verify the district for any specific address (how), and check internet availability, which changes street-by-street.
