The honest take

Why it's on the list: "2 months free" is the biggest advertised concession in our coverage area — deeper than anything in the Willow Park pocket — on 1-bedrooms starting around $1,115. On paper that's a net-effective rent in the mid-$900s, which is remarkable for a community on the I-20 frontage.

What to check: concessions this size are a signal to slow down, not speed up. Ask the three questions that decide whether the deal is real: which lease lengths qualify (deep offers often require 13–15 months), which months the free rent actually lands in (month one vs spread changes your budget completely), and what the renewal picture looks like — because year two is negotiated from the $1,115 base, not the effective rate. The fine-print guide covers the full checklist.
