The honest take

Why it's on the list: Chapel Creek Cottages is the west-FW answer to the Willow Park build-to-rent products — gated cottage-style rentals with yards, 2-bedrooms from ~$2,025 and 3-bedrooms from ~$2,285, without the Parker County drive. No shared walls, closer to Lockheed/NAS JRB than anything comparable we track.

What to check: no weeks-free special is currently advertised, which changes the comparison shape: Canvas at ~$2,321 with 6 weeks free nets out near $2,090 effective — cheaper than it looks against Chapel Creek's $2,285 3BR. Run both through the calculator, then weigh the commute savings against the school-district question — verify the zoned campus for any specific unit before the district factor decides anything.
