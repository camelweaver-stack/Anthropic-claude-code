Canvas at Willow Park: renting a house, not an apartment

A 110-home build-to-rent neighborhood in Aledo ISD — and a case study in why you always get the special quoted in writing.

✓ Compiled from public listings: July 2026 — offers change weekly; confirm with the leasing office before you tour

What it is

Canvas isn't a normal apartment complex — it's a build-to-rent neighborhood: 110 standalone 2-, 3-, and 4-bedroom homes (built 2022, managed by Willow Bridge) with farmhouse-style exteriors, attached two-car garages, private yards, and no upstairs neighbors. You're renting a house with a clubhouse, pool, and gym attached. It sits at 300 Meadow Place Dr — literally next door to Gates at Meadow Place — in Parker County, zoned to Aledo ISD.

The special — and a lesson in fine print

Here's something instructive: as of this writing, three major listing sites advertise three different Canvas specials — "up to 6 weeks free," "8 weeks free," and "up to 2 months free." All three can be simultaneously true, because offers vary by floor plan, move-in date, and lease length — the promotional rates appear tied to 14+ month leases. This is exactly why we say it on every page: get the current offer for your unit and your dates quoted in writing before you apply.

Honest fit

Choose Canvas if: you need bedrooms and a garage, you want Aledo ISD without buying yet, and a yard matters (pets: allowed, $500 fee + $40/mo, 2-pet limit). The known costs: $125 app fee, deposits advertised around $250. Look elsewhere if: you're a one-person household — there are no 1-bedrooms here at all; Olympus, half a mile away, starts around $1,264 and runs its own 8-weeks-free offer. The comparison that matters: a Canvas 3-bedroom near $2,349 is deep in own-it territory — that payment supports roughly $275K of house in Texas math. If your credit is solid, run your number before signing a 14-month term.
