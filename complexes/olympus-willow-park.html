The honest take

Why it's on my list: that 1-bedroom price with 8 weeks free is one of the strongest net-effective deals in the Willow Park pocket right now — for a newer community in a school-district area, the math is genuinely good. What to check before you fall in love: concessions this size usually mean active lease-up, so ask about construction timeline, amenity completion, and what renewal increases have looked like — the discount year and the renewal year are two different rents. Run the net-effective comparison against Gates at Meadow Place and Canvas before you tour: total 12-month cost ÷ 12, per property.
