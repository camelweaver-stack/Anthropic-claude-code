The honest take

Why it's on my list: Olympus is the pocket's bellwether — and this month it's the market's clearest signal: the advertised offer went from up-to-8 weeks free in August to 2 weeks on the September 3 price-verified listing, while the 1-bedroom sticker climbed from ~$1,264 to $1,425. The deep-discount lease-up phase here looks over. What to check: stale listings still advertise the old 8-week offer, so get the current offer for your unit quoted in writing before you tour — and ask what renewal increases have looked like, because the discount year and the renewal year are two different rents. Run the net-effective comparison against Gates at Meadow Place and the Hudson Oaks sibling (still at up to 6 weeks) before deciding: total 12-month cost ÷ 12, per property.
