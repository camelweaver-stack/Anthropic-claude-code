The West Side Relocation Guide

The checklist edition — bookmark it; your email link always points at the current version.

60+ days out

□ Pick your pocket: renter quiz or buyer math · □ Verify the zoned campus for any specific address (how) · □ Remote worker? Confirm internet at the address · □ Set the budget with real numbers: income check (renting) or affordability at local tax rates (buying) · □ Military: BAH fit tables and the HEAT housing waitlist early.

30 days out

□ Tour week = quote week: specials decay mid-month; get unit, rate, special, and itemized fees in ONE written email · □ Total the real cash: move-in calculator · □ Compare offers on net-effective only: the 30-second math · □ Buying? Calendar the option period the day the contract executes (the clock) · □ Renters insurance quote ($15–30/mo) — most leases require it at signing.

Move week

□ Electricity: pick a plan at powertochoose.org (it's choose-your-own in this corridor) · □ Ask how water is billed — submetered vs allocated (why it matters) · □ Photograph EVERYTHING at move-in and submit the condition form — it's the whole deposit fight, settled in advance · □ Buying? File the $140K homestead exemption the week you close.
