Renting, but ready

Paying $1,700+ with steady work at Alliance? Rent and ownership costs can be put side by side — but what a payment actually buys depends on the rate, the down payment, taxes, insurance, and any MUD/PID on the lot. The calculator runs your number as a labeled scenario, not a rule of thumb.

The core idea

Rent and ownership are comparable numbers — once the full costs are on the table. Some months renting genuinely wins — especially mid-relocation. Some leases quietly cost a down payment. What a monthly payment supports in ownership depends materially on financing and the property itself: rate, term, down payment, taxes, insurance, HOA, and any MUD/PID. Our whole job is putting those numbers side by side, in arithmetic you can check — before you sign anything.
