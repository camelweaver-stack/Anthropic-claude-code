Buyer's field guide · Keller

Buying in Keller: the pocket playbook.

Published by Anastasia Weaver Media · Independent local research, no listings, no pay-for-placement · Updated August 16, 2026 · Methodology & corrections

Same corridor, different regime — here's the Keller version of the buyer's game.

The local read

Buying in Keller means buying resale, and resale here means the corridor's deepest stock of mature-tree, larger-lot neighborhoods. The inspection conversation replaces the design-center conversation: roof, HVAC, and foundation history are your line items, and homes with documented service records earn their asking prices. Because new-construction competition is distant, Keller sellers negotiate on classic terms — which means your leverage comes from preparation and comparables, not from a model home down the street.

Run the standard plays

Everywhere on the corridor: district by address, total tax rate (with MUD/PID) in writing, commute driven at your real hours, and every incentive priced as cash. The full comparison framework lives in new build vs. resale; the pocket's character in the Keller profile. Compiled August 2026; not a valuation.
