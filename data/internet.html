CHECK BEFORE YOU SIGN — COVERAGE IS STREET-BY-STREET

Internet options across the west FW corridor

Out here, fiber on one street becomes fixed-wireless two blocks over — and for remote workers that difference matters more than most amenities on the brochure. Here's what's actually available by city, and the two questions to ask any community before you sign.

The two questions that outrank any coverage map

1. "What is this building actually wired for?" A community inside a fiber footprint isn't automatically fiber-served — the line has to reach the building and the unit. Newer build-to-rent communities are typically pre-wired for at least one high-speed provider; older properties may only have coax. Ask which providers serve the unit, and ask a current resident if you can.

2. "Is internet bundled, exclusive, or my choice?" Some communities have bulk agreements — internet billed with rent through one mandated provider. That's convenient or captive depending on the provider and price; either way it belongs in your monthly math alongside the rest of the utility picture.

Remote worker rule of thumb: confirm the upload speed, not just download — fiber's symmetrical uploads are why it matters for video calls — and test a 5G Home hotspot at the actual unit during your tour if fiber isn't available.
