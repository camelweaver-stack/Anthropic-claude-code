The entertainment file: what there is to do

A portrait painter's secret garden, a classic-country opry house, a museum with Larry Hagman's mother in it, and a movie theater with a veteran's discount — the corridor's entertainment, verified.

The homegrown circuit

Chandor Gardens (711 W Lee Ave, Weatherford — $5) is the corridor's best-kept non-secret: three acres of English-meets-Asian gardens built in the 1930s by portrait artist Douglas Chandor, hidden at the end of a residential street. Clark Gardens (Maddux Rd, west of town) is the bigger botanical sibling — wedding-famous, with a miniature train and genuine peak-bloom seasons. The Doss Heritage and Culture Center (1400 Texas Dr) carries the county's story — including native daughter Mary Martin (Broadway's Peter Pan, and yes, Larry Hagman's mother) — with log cabins out back and family-friendly programming. The Texas Opry Theater (319 York Ave) runs classic-country and tribute shows in an intimate room — "ol' timey rock and roll without the hoochie coo," per one satisfied local. City Lights (1000 Cinema Dr, Hudson Oaks) is the corridor's movie house — old-school, matinee-priced, veteran and military discounts — with Weatherford's bowling-and-movies entertainment center as the rainy-day alternative and Bush League's arcade next door for the after-show.

Live music, night out, and the city tier

Live music runs weekly without a cover: The District's Lucchese pavilion (several nights), Railhead's post-game stage, and Parker County Brewing's patio calendar. Date-night tiers: the corridor handles casual-through-nice (Dayne's, Boo-Ray's, Press Café, Mesquite Pit — the rankings); Clearfork's row is the dress-up answer 20 minutes east; and the full city tier — the Cultural District's museums, Dickies Arena's concert calendar, Bass Hall, the Stockyards — sits 25–35 minutes out, close enough for a school-night show. The seasonal layer — Peach Festival to Christmas on the Square — is the calendar's department, and the free stuff (trails, the lakes, four libraries, Friday lights) is arguably the strongest entertainment budget line of all.
