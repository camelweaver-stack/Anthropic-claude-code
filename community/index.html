How this desk works

Everything here follows the house rules: places verified against posted hours, events against organizers' published dates, no paid placements, and a dated stamp on every page. Rankings are opinions — ours, argued in the open — and they get re-walked every season, because the west side keeps opening new doors. This desk launched with its founding dispatches in Summer 2026 and builds monthly from here; the list gets each new dispatch first. En español: la Mesa Comunitaria.
