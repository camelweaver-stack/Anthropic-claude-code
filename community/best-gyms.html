BEST OF THE WEST · RANKED & REVISITED SEASONALLY

The best gyms on the west side, ranked

From a 24/7 bodybuilder's haven in Hudson Oaks to the group-training room with a perfect rating — ranked by training style, because 'best gym' depends entirely on who's asking.

The rankings, by how you train

Best for serious lifters: No Fear Fitness — Hudson Oaks (2602 Fort Worth Hwy, open 24/7). Equipment you'd otherwise drive to Dallas for, a recovery room with sauna and posing space, and a perfect local rating from a clientele that includes traveling competitors who plan vacations around it. Best full-service: Texas Health Fitness Center — Willow Park (101 Crown Pointe Blvd, 5am weekdays). The classes-pool-and-trainers option attached to the medical cluster, including the N.E.A.T. program for cancer patients and survivors — the most complete facility west of the loop, with the honest caveat that longtime members grumble about equipment upkeep. Best community: F45 Willow Park (The District, from 5am daily) — group training with a flawless 5.0 across nearly a hundred reviews, coach-led form work, and the accountability engine people actually stick with. Best owner-run: Powerhouse Gym — Weatherford (1800 Lone Oak Rd) — veteran-owned, immaculate, member-polled equipment purchases; the trade-off is limited hours and a single bathroom per side. The daily-life geography of all four — plus the trails that make the fifth gym free — is mapped in the family guide's services layer.

Verified against public listings, posted hours, and event organizers' published information, July 2026. Independent coverage — no placements are paid, ever. Rankings are this desk's editorial judgment and get revisited every season.
